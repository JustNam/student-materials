# Elements
## Outcomes
- A Working Product, which is available for scalability
- Student's Thorough Understanding of The Product
## Scoping Standards
- The solution has to cover web/mobile FE, BE, and database
	- Negative examples: Chrome extension, internal tool...
- The problem the product solves should be grounded in validated data points
- Database should not have too many/too few database tables; the number of database tables should be around 5 to 10 tables
## Settings
- Basic Settings
	- Duration: 2 weeks
	- Teamwork: 2 members
	- Inputs: 
		- Your well-researched topics from Starter. Their scope will be tailored by the mentor to prevent overwhelming them
		- Sample/Architecture file from CLRA
- Sequential Process: each milestone will end with oral defenses
	1. Finding fundamental misfits (2 Days)
	2. MVP (6 days) 
	3. Iteration with swapped roles (6 days)
- Daily Process: 
	- Daily teach-back ritual: in each 20 min session, the "owner" has 10 mins to walk their partner through what they built; the partner exposes what the owner has not understood yet
## Best practices
- Spending a small amount of time on programming manually to avoid over-delegating implementation to AI
- If the plan is too overwhelming (reading 30% of the plan but not understanding shit), break it down into multiple increments
- Timebox each feature and make it fit; don't hallucinate features not based on misfit. YAGNI!
- When writing the external docs by hand, you could keep them in the repo directly, but don't let AI touch them
